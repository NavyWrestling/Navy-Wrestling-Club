May 7, 2026

Coach Barry Davis Announces Retirement

After a decorated 33-year coaching career, Navy assistant wrestling coach Barry Davis has announced his retirement to spend more time with his family.

Navy (2024–2026): Served as an assistant coach, making a significant impact in recruiting and mentoring. He helped the Midshipmen secure a share of the 2024-25 EIWA regular-season title and guided multiple athletes to All-American honors.

Wisconsin (25 years): Served as head coach and became the program's all-time winningest coach with a 227-167-11 record, leading the Badgers to 15 top-20 NCAA finishes and three national champions. Named 2010 National Coach of the Year.

Iowa (1986–1992): Began his coaching career as a graduate and assistant coach, helping the Hawkeyes win three NCAA Championships and seven consecutive Big Ten titles.

As a competitor, Barry was a three-time NCAA Champion and four-time Big Ten Champion at Iowa, won a silver medal at the 1984 Olympics, gold at the 1983 Pan American Games, and was inducted into the National Wrestling Hall of Fame in 2007.

March 28, 2025

Two All-Americans, 21st-Place Finish for the Mids

Navy wrestlers Josh Koderhandt (141 lbs) and Danny Wask (174 lbs) capped off the 2024-25 season by earning All-America honors at the 94th NCAA Division I Wrestling Championships in Philadelphia. Koderhandt finished sixth and Wask eighth — the first pair of Navy wrestlers to earn All-America status in the same season since 2009.

Their performances helped Navy finish 21st as a team, the program's best showing since 2016. Koderhandt, a senior team captain, finished his career with 106 total wins (tied for 13th all-time in Navy history) and 31 career pins (sixth all-time). Wask, a sophomore, became the fifth Navy wrestler to earn All-America honors at 174 lbs.

November 25, 2024

Navy Classic Recap

The 46th Annual Navy Classic returned to Wesley Brown Field House in Annapolis. The Midshipmen finished with eight athletes placing in the top five and one champion: 3/C Daniel Wask repeated as 174 lb champion for the second straight year, an honor shared by only 17 wrestlers in Naval Academy program history. Navy finished 3rd as a team.

September 18, 2024

Blue vs. Gold Weekend

Alumni and families gathered for Blue vs. Gold Weekend — a Friday social at the Federal House followed by the Blue vs. Gold wrestling match at Dahlgren Hall, then a Saturday tailgate and Navy Football game. The weekend remains one of the Club's signature events for keeping generations of Navy wrestlers connected.

July 1, 2024

New Director of Operations: Ben Barton

Navy Wrestling welcomed Ben Barton as the new Director of Operations for the Midshipmen. Ben joins after working with Rudis Wrestling Apparel, and previously qualified twice for the Division I NCAA Tournament with Campbell University and once with Lock Haven University, where he also earned a Master's degree in sports administration.

June 24, 2024

Camp, Camp, Camp — Summer 2024

Summer 2024 Navy Wrestling Camps kicked off with the Father/Son-Daughter Camp on Father's Day weekend, followed by a Technique Camp drawing over 200 campers, the SEALs Elite Camp focused on physical and mental toughness, and a season-closing Team Competition Camp where athletes wrestled up to 30 matches against competitors from across the country.

May 7, 2024

May 7th Ambassador Meeting

Former Navy Wrestling alumni and fans met on a Zoom call to strategize the future of the NWC, Navy Wrestling, and avenues for support. With over 30 participants, the session opened with Coach Kolat's season review and a Q&A covering the program's trajectory, the influence of NIL, and four new staff hires.

Coach Kolat Recaps 2023–24 Season

April 12, 2024

"If Not Me, Then Who" — Grady Griess Receives Travis Manion Award

Presented by the Trustees, Athletic and Scholarship Programs of the U.S. Naval Academy Foundation, the Travis Manion Award honors the graduating Navy wrestler who displayed outstanding leadership and work habits throughout his career — aggressiveness, determination, good sportsmanship, and compassion for others, putting team success ahead of personal achievement.
