Train With Us

Join a Training Session

The Navy Wrestling Club is committed to mentoring, improving, and instilling values in athletes of all ages. We offer Folkstyle and Freestyle training sessions for 8th grade and up within a 250-mile radius of Annapolis.

Folkstyle Training

NWC coaches focus on folkstyle development each preseason, led by our experienced staff. Practices are held in the USNA Wrestling Room, Lejeune Hall, and are free to attend. Each athlete must have a valid USA Wrestling Card to participate — check the calendar on our Updates page for the current schedule.

Interested in learning from some of the best wrestlers and coaches in the country? The NWC offers individual and group clinics led by our staff. Reach out through our Contact page to schedule a session with any of the coaches below.

Where We Train

Our Facility

Club members have the exclusive opportunity to train at the United States Naval Academy grounds, known as "the Yard." The wrestling room features three mats, stationary bikes, a dedicated weight room, and a spectator catwalk.

Be Part of the Room

Donor support keeps the Yard's wrestling room running, coached, and open to the next generation.
