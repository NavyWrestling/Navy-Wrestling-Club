About the Club

A nonprofit founded and run by former Naval Academy wrestlers, dedicated to mentoring and coaching the next generation of Midshipmen athletes.

Our Mission

Elevating Athletes. Developing Leaders.

The Navy Wrestling Club is dedicated to elevating the sport by providing elite amateur athletes in our region with the resources required to train and excel at the highest levels of competition. We prioritize engagement with our dedicated alumni and supporters to foster a lifelong brotherhood built on shared values and resilience. In partnership with our supporters, we build the foundation for these young men to succeed on the mat, in their academic pursuits, and ultimately as future officers and leaders in our nation.

Our History

From the Blue and Gold Club to Today

The Navy Wrestling Club began as the Blue and Gold Club, formed by Naval Academy wrestling alumni who wanted to give back to the program that shaped them. Over time it grew into a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, with a clear focus: fund the gap between what the Naval Academy provides and what it takes to compete at a national championship level.

Today the Club funds coaching positions, travel and competition costs, training equipment, and alumni programming that keeps generations of Navy wrestlers connected. Every dollar donated goes directly back into the program — there is no overhead layer between donors and Midshipmen.

Through the Years

Four head coaches who built the Navy Wrestling Club. Tap an era for the details.

Current Era2020 – Present▸

To date, the club has raised over $680,000 toward our annual operational budget and launched a long-term initiative with a $5 million goal to ensure the enduring sustainability of our elite training environment. Strategically, our priorities have evolved to focus on recruiting elite, weight-class-specific training partners. This targeted investment directly elevates the competitive standard of the room, with the ultimate goal of developing athletes capable of reaching the podium at the highest tiers of national competition.

Coaching Staff

Meet the Coaches

Donor support funds elite-level coaching, helping Navy compete at the highest levels of collegiate wrestling.

Head Coach

Cary Kolat

A two-time NCAA champion at Lock Haven and 2000 Olympian, Kolat won a silver and bronze medal on the U.S. Freestyle team and was inducted into the National Wrestling Hall of Fame in 2017. He now leads the Navy program as head coach.

Assistant Coach

Barry Davis

A three-time NCAA champion at Iowa (1982-84) and 1984 Olympic silver medalist, Davis spent 25 years as head coach at Wisconsin before joining Navy, bringing a philosophy built on discipline and technical excellence.

Assistant Coach

Blaize Cabell

Now in his fifth season at Navy, Cabell coaches the 184/197/285-lb classes and guided David Key to an 8th-place NCAA finish — Navy's first All-American under Cary Kolat. A 3x NCAA qualifier at Northern Iowa.

Assistant Coach

Quinn Kinner

A two-time New Jersey state champion who went on to wrestle at Ohio State and Rider, Kinner earned 2024 NCAA All-American honors by finishing 8th at the national tournament.

Assistant Coach

Matthew Hicks

Son of former Navy NCAA runner-up Wayne Hicks, Matt grew up around the program through Navy Juniors before returning as a coach, having wrestled at Bloomsburg University.

Assistant Coach

Mack Lewnes

Cornell's all-time winningest wrestler at 150-12, a 3x All-American, and a 4-year EIWA champion — one of only 9 wrestlers in over 100 years to do so. Brings elite competitive pedigree to the Navy staff.

Assistant Coach

Peyton Walsh

A 2015 Naval Academy graduate and former team captain, Walsh returned to Annapolis in 2024 after nine years in the Marine Corps, where he earned a Pan-American gold medal and was a 2020 Tokyo Olympic alternate.

Assistant Coach

Zac Braunagel

A 2025 Illinois graduate, 5-time NCAA qualifier, and 2x Big Ten bronze medalist, Braunagel competed for Team USA on the 2023 World Team and 2024-25 National Team, finishing 3rd at the 2024 Olympic Team Trials.
