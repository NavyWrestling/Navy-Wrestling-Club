Support the Club

The Navy Wrestling Club strengthens the wrestling community by providing the resources required for elite competitors to excel at the highest level. The club prioritizes engagement with alumni and supporters to strengthen our lifelong brotherhood. In partnership with elite supporters and alumni, we build the foundation for these young men to succeed on the mat, in the classroom, as Navy and Marine Corps officers, and as leaders in our nation.

This Year's Campaign

We Need Your Help

Each year, the Navy Wrestling Club has grown more and more support from our alumni and fanbase. The increased support has allowed us to hire new coaches, purchase new training equipment, host social events, and participate in off-season training and competition events. The Navy Wrestling Club has set a new fundraising goal this year with the hopes of hiring another coach and training partner to aid in the development of these young men on and off the mat. We have also increased our annual fundraising goal to $300k to help us continue the alumni engagement progress we've made over the last several years… AND WE NEED YOUR HELP!

This year, we would like to thank all our new and returning supporters with some Navy Wrestling Club apparel. Become a recurring donor of $50/month or provide a one-time donation of $600 to receive a pair of Navy Wrestling Club T-shirts, OR become a recurring donor of $100/month or provide a one-time donation of $1,200 to receive both T-shirts and an Under Armour quarter-zip.

Your Impact

Where Your Gift Goes

Coaching & Staff

Donor support has directly funded coaching hires including Barry Davis, a 1984 Olympic silver medalist, and Director of Operations Ben Barton — giving our Midshipmen elite-level development.

Competition & Travel

In 2025, Navy placed 21st at the NCAA Championships — the program's best finish since 2016 — with two All-Americans. Your gift funds travel and entry fees to put wrestlers on the national stage.

Alumni Brotherhood

From Blue vs. Gold Weekend to Ambassador Meetings, the NWC connects generations of Navy wrestlers. Donations fund events that keep the Navy Wrestling family strong for life.

Equipment & Facilities

Training gear, mat upgrades, and facilities at Wesley Brown Field House give our wrestlers every competitive advantage — on the mat and in the weight room.
